# What is World4?

World4 is a **physical accounting model of world consumption**. It answers one
question, mechanically: *if a sector produces less, what happens — everywhere — to
the environment and to human work?*

It is **not** an economic forecast. There are no prices, no markets, no behaviour
prediction. We assume a planner can set activity levels directly, and that the
world's productive capital is **frozen in its current physical state**. The seed
only ever does **less** — it is a *degrowth at constant apparatus* sandbox.

## How a change propagates

Every product depends on others (steel needs energy, energy needs water…). World4
uses a global Multi-Regional Input-Output table (**EXIOBASE**, 49 regions × 200
products) and the **Leontief inverse** to trace those dependencies. When you reduce
the final demand for a product, the model computes the full upstream ripple across
all countries:

> `Δoutput = L · Δdemand` (reductions only) → `Δimpact = intensity · Δoutput`

## Two halves, equal weight

- **Ecology** — carbon, water, land, materials, energy embodied in consumption.
- **Human work** — the hours of labour an economy requires, and how a society can
  share them (retirement age, working hours, employment rate).

Both are shown side by side, each with its own unit. **There is no single "score"** —
combining incommensurable things into one index would hide more than it reveals.
