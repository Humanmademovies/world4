# Glossary

**MRIO (Multi-Regional Input-Output table)** — a map of the world economy: who buys
what from whom, across every country and sector. World4 uses EXIOBASE 3 (49 regions,
200 products, year 2022).

**Final demand** — goods/services consumed by end users (households, government,
investment, exports), as opposed to intermediate sales between industries. Levers act
on final demand.

**Leontief inverse (L)** — the matrix that turns a change in final demand into the
total (direct + indirect) change in production across the whole supply chain.

**Footprint / consumption-based** — impacts attributed to the country that *finally
consumes* a good, including those embodied in imports. (Production-based, by contrast,
counts impacts where they physically occur.)

**Production hours** — hours of labour worked *in* a country to produce its output;
the basis of the Work-time panel.

**Coverage** — how well EXIOBASE maps an impact. Carbon, water, materials, land are
well covered; some stressors are missing (shown honestly, never faked).

**Overshoot** — a footprint divided by its fair-share boundary; >1 means over budget.

**Degrowth at constant apparatus** — the seed's stance: explore doing *less* with
today's capital, without prices, substitution, or new "green" investment.
