# Sustainability targets

For each limit, the **Targets** panel compares a country's **per-capita footprint**
to a **sourced fair-share boundary**, and shows the **overshoot** (how many times
over, and the day of the year the annual budget runs out).

## Where the numbers come from

- **O'Neill et al. 2018** — equal-per-capita, consumption-based planetary boundaries:
  **1.61 tCO₂**, **574 m³** blue water, **7.2 t** materials per person per year.
- **Hot or Cool** — 1.5° lifestyle carbon targets: **2.5 / 0.7 tCO₂e** for 2030 / 2050.

The **carbon ambition** selector switches between these.

## Honest by design

- **No aggregate index** — each limit keeps its own metric, side by side.
- **No invented targets** — **land** and **energy** have no clean per-capita planetary
  boundary, so they are shown *without* a target rather than with a made-up line.
- The **allocation rule** is equal-per-capita (a deliberate, stated choice, not the
  only possible one), and the **footprint** (rigorous) is kept visually distinct from
  the **target** (sourced but contestable).

## Reading it

A ratio above 1 (red) means the country is over its fair share for that limit; below
1 (green) means within it. Hover a row for the exact source.
