# Epistemic framing — never lose sight of this

This is the contract for what World4's **seed** is and is not. Every feature is
judged against it. (Source: `instructions_projet.md`.)

## It is a physical model, not a behavioural one

"If a sector drops, what happens *mechanically*, everywhere?" There are **no
prices, no elasticities, no market equilibrium, no behaviour prediction**. The
scenario postulates a world governance that directly sets activity levels.
Productive capital is **frozen in its current physical state**.

## It is pure degrowth at constant apparatus

We close the sustainability gap by doing **less**, never by investing in "green"
capital. No substitution, no transition-by-construction of new capital. **Any
activity increase is out of scope for the seed.** (Growth hypotheses — fusion,
asteroid mining, wind-fleet expansion — belong to the later game layers, never
bolted onto the accounting seed.)

## Three framing choices, respected strictly

1. **Levers = demand; engine = Leontief.** A lever reduces the final use of a
   product; the Leontief inverse propagates the drop upstream (suppliers, in all
   countries). Feedback is **negative only** — we never postulate an increase.
   The Ghosh model (downstream propagation / input shortage) and the direct
   closure of a specific sector-country are **out of the seed** (deferred).
2. **No freed income/labour reinjected.** Physical economy: the quantity of goods
   in circulation cannot rise. Cutting a sector finances nothing elsewhere. No
   positive `Δdemand` is ever added — this is the default behaviour, nothing to
   code.
3. **Tunable sustainability targets, per limit.** Overshoot day parameterised;
   carbon budget, planetary-boundary thresholds and their per-capita downscaling
   are **sourced parameters**, never hardcoded. Ambition sliders: 1.5 °C / 2 °C /
   impact-neutral / regenerative. **Never a single aggregate index** — each limit
   is shown side by side with its own metric.

## Anti-mathwashing

Any assumption that could hide in the engine must become an **explicit, sourced
slider with an uncertainty range**. Canonical example: "what fraction of final
demand is unnecessary / advertising-driven" is not a model fact — it is a
parameter the user sets. We never present a result that depends on a contested
assumption with the false rigour of an accounting calculation. The rigour of the
seed (Leontief) and the uncertainty of usage parameters must be **visually
distinct**. See [parameters.md](parameters.md).

## Honest, uneven impact coverage

Carbon / water / materials / land have good economy→pressure mapping.
Biodiversity, novel entities (chemistry), N/P flows have lacunar mapping →
**show the real coverage, never fake a uniform one.**

## Validation is binary

At each milestone, reproduce a **published** figure (e.g. France's per-capita
carbon/water footprint) before advancing. Numbers are cited, never invented.

## Explicitly out of the seed (deferred, do not engineer without an explicit ask)

These are separate objects; grafting them onto the accounting seed would break it
or manufacture false precision:

- **Supply side / closing a specific sector-country** (Ghosh model or reallocation
  layer).
- **Health co-benefits** ("earlier retirement → health"). At most an optional
  qualitative badge, never a central calculation.
- **Dynamic-ludic layer** (year-by-year projection; happiness, inequality →
  wars/famines/revolutions; "collapse by 2100" loss condition). This is a
  **separate second model in the System-Dynamics / World3 lineage** plugged onto
  the seed — not an extension of it. Reserved for the game phase.
